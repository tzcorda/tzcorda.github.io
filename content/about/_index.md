##Tiziana Corda Research Fellow Unimi University of Milan Github

---
I am a **{{< colourbold >}}researcher{{< /colourbold >}}** at the Department of Social and Political Sciences of the **{{< colourbold >}}University of Milan{{< /colourbold >}}**. ​My main areas of research are authoritarian politics, regime transformations including democratization / autocratization processes, and the effects foreign pressure such as aid and sanctions has on these trajectories, with a predominant focus on Africa. Currently, I am co-leading a work package in the **{{< colourbold >}}Horizon project RESISTANCE{{< /colourbold >}}** [◎](https://europeandemocracyhub.epd.eu/resistance/), aiming at understanding how interscalar authoritarian networks are assembled, how they gain appeal worldwide, and how they can be countered. I am also co-chair of the Standing Group on Political Regimes at SISP (Italian Political Science Association) and an Associate Research Fellow at ISPI (Italian Institute for International Political Studies). 

My academic journey has also taken me to Sciences Po (2024–2025) and the University of Pau (2024–2027) as an **{{< colourbold >}}adjunct professor{{< /colourbold >}}**, and to IDOS, the German Institute of Development and Sustainability (2025), and the University of Groningen (2020), as a **{{< colourbold >}}visiting researcher{{< /colourbold >}}**. In 2020-2022, I participated in a research project funded by the European Research Council (ERC) and led by the University of Sussex on Violence, Elites, and Resilience in States Under Stress (VERSUS), focusing mostly on Ethiopia’s political crises and conflicts. In 2022, I received a PhD in Political Studies, with a dissertation on the internal decision-making processes in countries targeted by international sanctions. Prior to my PhD, I worked three years on global policy issues for the Africa Programme of ISPI, the EU’s European External Action Service (EEAS), and the Scalabrini Centre of Cape Town (SCCT).

**{{< colourbold >}}Now based in Italy{{< /colourbold >}}**. Long ago, also in Brussels, Tehran, and Cape Town.
